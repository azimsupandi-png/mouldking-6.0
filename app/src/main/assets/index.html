Joystick

Tilt steering

Touch and hold the joystick with one thumb. Up and down drives. Tilt the phone left or right like a steering wheel to steer. It works with the left joystick, or the first joystick on the Board. Hold the phone upright or sideways, not flat.

Sequences

Each sequence becomes a quick action button. Steps run one after another; tick "together" to start a step with the one above.

Gamepad

No gamepad found. Pair it in Android Bluetooth settings, then press any button.

Button mapping

Left stick = stick 1, right stick = stick 2 (Dual stick layout). Back = E-Stop, Start = release. The sticks use the channels chosen in the stick cards.

Calibration

Runs each channel forward at the test power for 1.5 seconds. Lift the model off the ground first. Say whether it moved the right way and the app fixes the direction for you.

Used by Calibration and Test hub. Ignores Limit and Speed, so raise it until the motor just moves.
